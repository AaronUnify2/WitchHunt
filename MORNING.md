## What's new

1. **The whole story spine is fixed:** the same place on every seed. Debug, Teleport & map, "next story place" walks you through them in story order:
   - the crashed plane, just past the checkpoint (the checkpoint's path leads straight to it);
   - the clearing crew's dozer, at the east end of the cordon strip;
   - the old church;
   - the stairs, off the paths: follow the faint trail;
   - the crashed UFO, the Car Armory and Ground Zero;
   - the witch's hut.

   Each one is a signpost for now. The witch's routes go round them.
2. **Optional encounters:** the seed deals 3 or 4 of the five designed ones each run and puts them somewhere around the spine. The railway cutting always sits on the railway.
3. **The road north:** it forks off about 75 m west of the gas station. A fallen oak blocks it on Day 1.
   - At the top is the old **Laurel Gap Tunnel (W.P.A. 1936)**. It has fallen in, and the army's **ROAD CLOSED** barrier stands in front of it, with its lamps still blinking.
   - Just south of the barrier is a spur of rock with **Bigfoot's cave** behind thick brush. On foot you can't get through: "It would take a machine…". Drive the dozer's blade into it and the brush tears away to show the cave mouth. The map stops calling the place "Road's end".
   - Earl's **BIGFOOT XING** and **HOME OF BIGFOOT** signs are on the way up, and there are big prints by the brush. Is that too much of a hint? (#363, #365)
4. **The 1903 railway** runs across the whole map between the middle and south bands. It's overgrown, the rails are rusted, and the telegraph poles lean.
   - For "have some fun": the road never reaches the railway. Instead, every path that crosses it gets an old crossing: planks between the rails, crossbucks, a **LOOK OUT FOR THE CARS** board and whistle posts.
   - At each edge of the map there's a stone tunnel with **1903** on its keystone, fallen in.
   - An idea I didn't build: a pump handcar you can ride along the line (#367).
5. **Interiors:** the storm shelter's room is the first one.
   - It loads as you climb down. The forest isn't drawn while you're inside, and the world carries on outside.
   - It has its own dim light, and you can see the sky up the shaft.
   - Top left shows the time and how long until dusk.
   - A save made down there brings you back beside the hatch.
   - The cave, the place beyond the stairs and the mothership will use the same system.
