## What to play

1. **The yard.** Two black sedans across the mill road. One has its boot open on quicklime, shovels and a roll of tarp. The last agent sits dead against the other with the radio handset in his lap; the radio's still talking. The SMG is beside him.
2. **The bunkhouse.** Walk in. The bunks are empty. Look at the case on the card table, then follow the drag marks out of the door.
3. **The row under the tarps, and the pit.** You can't open the tarps.
4. **The mill shed.** The head saw has a log stopped in the cut. There's a man in a suit on the floor with a chainsaw stood in him, and another under the logs off the deck.
5. **The filing room** on the shed's end: the foreman's day book is on his desk. Seven pages.
6. **Work the chainsaw out** (hold X), then rev it (hold RT). That's what wakes them: the tarps sit up one after another, then the night shift, then the agents.
7. **Knock the head saw off its arbor** (hold X at the head rig). It's the killdozer's saw one day.
8. **At home:** the workbench is in the main room on your left as you come in. It sharpens the chain for 3 scrap.

## The chainsaw, in short

- Hold RT. It spins up for half a second, then cuts for as long as you hold it: about a third of a second per citizen, two at once.
- It's loud (everything within 55 m comes), it slows you to 60% (42% in a cut), and it overheats after about 9 s of revving or 5 s of cutting. Then it stalls for about 5 s.
- The chain dulls over about 50 s of cutting. Dull, it does a third of the damage. HEAT and EDGE bars sit under the weapon's name.
