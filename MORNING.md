## What to play

**PLAYTEST rounds 64-66.** The quickest way in: debug menu, **Story, radio & trail cams**, "Holler: take me there". It puts you before the mouth, walking in.

1. **The mouth.** Walk in: a sweep up the hollow. Two wooded ridges, a creek, and the first trap already sprung, with a man in a black suit face down in front of it.
2. **The traps up the floor.** You get a line the first time you come near each kind:
   - fishing line across the floor, tied to a shotgun on a stump;
   - tin cans on a wire;
   - leaves piled too neat (a bear trap);
   - a sapling bent over a noose (a snare).

   Jump the lines. Spring the bear traps and pull the snares' pegs with X. Or walk the dead into them.
3. **The lookout,** halfway up on the right. The wire cutters are in the tackle box (they cut the lines from then on). The family's trap map is on the board. It's out of date: two traps past the lookout aren't on it.
4. **The still's yard.** Five of the family stand about the still. They come when you get close, when the cans ring, or when you fire. Then the gauge starts climbing, and you have about two and a half minutes.
5. **Pa's board** on a post by the still gives the shutdown order: the damper, then the relief cock, then the line. Try one out of turn and see what happens. Let it blow once from a distance.
6. **The copper worm** comes out of the flake stand once the still's shut down. It's the dozer's flamethrower one day.
7. **The shed:** the auto shotgun in the gun cabinet, and shells in the ammo cans.
